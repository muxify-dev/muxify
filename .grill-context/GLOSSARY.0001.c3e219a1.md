+ Trade
  A Sidebar drag that takes a card across the Bench line and lets it go on a card on the other side. The two cards exchange their places in the Topics list, so one goes In Play and the other goes to the Bench. No other card moves.
  avoid: swap, Replace from Bench (when the replaced card does not go to the end of the Bench), exchange
  under: Topics
