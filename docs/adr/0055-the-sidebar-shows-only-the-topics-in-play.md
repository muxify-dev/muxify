# The Sidebar shows only the Topics In Play

When the Config sets `topics.in_play`, the Sidebar shows the Pinned Topics and the first N other cards, Topics or Locked Sessions. The other open Topics and Locked Sessions are the Bench, and they hide behind one line under the In Play cards. The goal is to limit how many Topics the user sees at one time. Each rule below keeps that limit in place and does not let it lose a Topic the user works on.

- **Pinned Topics do not count toward N.** A Pinned Topic always shows. Pin is how the user keeps a Topic in view, so the limit does not override it.
- **Locked Sessions count toward N.** A Locked Session is not a Topic, but its card takes the user's attention as a Topic's card does. A Locked Session cannot be Pinned, so it is In Play or Benched by its place in the list.
- **Only the user shows or hides the Bench.** Going to a Topic, In Play or Benched, leaves the Bench as it is. A user who leaves the Bench shown has a reason to, so going to a Topic In Play does not hide it.
- **The card of the Location always shows.** If that card is not In Play and the Bench is hidden, it shows alone under the line. This covers a Benched Topic or Benched Locked Session after a Command Palette jump, or after the user hides the Bench while on one. It also covers a No Topic Session that is not Locked. Its card shows only while the user is on it, so it does not count toward N. The card of the Location under the line, Benched or a No Topic Session, shows directly under the line, also when the Bench is shown: then it is above the first other Benched card. At its place in a long Bench, it would be out of view, and it would move each time the Bench shows or hides. Its place in the Topics list does not change. So, while the Bench is shown, a drag of a Benched card does not reorder it among the other Benched cards: they keep their places for each other. A jump does not show the whole Bench, because then every jump would show all the Topics again.
- **Hints and Topic steps go only to the cards that show.** With the Bench hidden, ⌃4 goes to the fourth card that shows, or does nothing. `select_next_topic` and `select_prev_topic` go through the cards that show and then wrap, so repeated steps do not go through the whole Bench. To reach a Benched Topic, the user shows the Bench or uses a Command Palette. The Next Topic after a Snooze or Settle still comes from the full list, because it replaces a Topic that leaves the list.
- **A drag across the line is a Trade.** A card let go on a card on the other side of the line exchanges places with it. No other card moves, so the line stays under the same number of cards. This is the rule while the Bench is shown and while it is hidden. A drag on one side of the line reorders the cards on that side. A card let go across the line but not on a card goes back, and nothing changes. A Pinned card is not In Play and not Benched, so it does not trade.
- **A drag shows the hidden Bench while it needs it.** An In Play card dragged down past the hidden line opens the Bench, so the user sees the cards it can trade with. The Bench hides again with no pause: at the drop, or as soon as two traded cards are in their new places. The card of the Location under the hidden line can trade with an In Play card. The card that it replaces lands under the line and folds into the hidden Bench at once. So the trade shows where each card went, the Bench does not stay open longer than the drag needs, and it stays as the user left it.
- **A Trade looks like an exchange, not a reorder.** While the dragged card is over a card it can trade with, that card has a dashed outline and a "⇄ Trade" mark, and the place the dragged card left shows that card, as "lands here". The dragged card steps aside, so the target stays in view. On the drop, the two cards pass each other on curved paths.
- **The line has no count and no Agent marks.** A number of waiting Topics, or a mark from one of them, pulls attention to the Bench, which the limit exists to avoid. There is no Config option to add them.
- **The Bench is in the Topics list's scroll view.** The line scrolls with the cards, and a shown Bench continues the same list. The "more below" line counts only the cards that show.

`toggle_bench` (default `shift+cmd+s`) and the Toggle Bench Command show and hide the Bench.

## Considered Options

A Trade replaced two earlier drag rules. First, a card dragged across the shown line changed places with the cards it passed, and the last In Play card or the first Benched card went the other way. Second, an In Play card could not go past the hidden line. With a Trade, the user chooses which card goes the other way, and the same rule works whether the Bench is shown or hidden. A Trade is not Replace from Bench: Replace from Bench puts the replaced card at the end of the Bench.

The line was chosen over four other designs: an "In Play 3/3" header with empty slots, a stack of card edges, a pill at the bottom of the Sidebar with one-line rows, and a dashed "visitor" card with a Put In Play Action. The line uses the "more below" line that the Sidebar already has, and it adds the fewest new states.
